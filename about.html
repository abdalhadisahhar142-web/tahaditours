Our story

A Middle East tour operator built on experience

Al Tahadi Travel & Tours is a Middle East tour operator and Destination Management Company, established in 2020 in Damascus, Syria, by a management team with more than twenty years of experience in the travel and tourism industry.

We are known for personal service, quality and value — backed by a comprehensive database and a professional team. Our aim is to provide complete services to groups and individual travellers, to and from the Middle East.

We aim to exceed our clients' expectations, and we follow their satisfaction closely on every trip.

Mission

To be recognised as a regional leader in the travel industry, and to build a platform with global recognition.
